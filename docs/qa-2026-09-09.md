## Chrome address bar "주의 요함"

Still a Pages setting, not code. Turn on Enforce HTTPS. Share only https://www.komitrando.com

## 1. Photos missing on mobile 4G

Patched in app.js.
Drive ImageURL now tries lh3 w800, then Drive thumbnail, then uc export=view.
Images load lazy with referrerpolicy=no-referrer.
Imgur URLs stay as-is.
If a row still fails on 4G, replace that ImageURL with Imgur or a GitHub raw file.

## 2. Card size and extra margin differ on phone vs iPad

Patched. Product photo stage is 1:1 on every breakpoint. object-fit contain inside the square.

## 3. Video play on two-up grids

Patched in product-video.js.
- Phone portrait, one card per row: mid-screen autoplay remains
- iPad / landscape two-up: tap the card to play, tap again to stop
- Desktop: hover play unchanged

## 4. Video seemed broken on PC, phone, and iPad

Cause: after wrapping cards in `.product-media`, the `<video>` was inserted *before* the still `<img>`, so the photo covered the clip. Audio/decode could run, but nothing was visible.

Fix (2026-09-09, product-video.js):
- Insert video after the image, z-index 2
- Fade the still to 0 while hovering or `.is-playing`
- Confirmed by user: works on PC, phone, and iPad
